QUESTIONS BEFORE YOU TRAVEL

A smoother start
begins with clarity.

These reminders help set expectations for the enquiry process.

Is sending the form a confirmed reservation?

No. It opens WhatsApp with your enquiry details. You must send the message and receive direct confirmation.

What if I do not know my exact pickup time?

Share your best estimate and explain the situation in your message. Confirm the final timing with the team.

Should I share my flight number?

For airport journeys, including your flight number can help clarify the arrival or departure you are enquiring about.
